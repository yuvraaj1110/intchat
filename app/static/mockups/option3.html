How many hours can I work on OPT?
✦ Assistant
On post-completion OPT, you can work full-time — there's no 20-hour weekly cap once your program is complete. During pre-completion OPT (while school is still in session), you're limited to 20 hours per week, and you can work full-time during official school breaks.
This is general guidance, not legal advice. Always confirm with your DSO or an immigration attorney.
